# Digital Archaeology

**Dig, Don't Hack.**

An evidence-first research program for historical smart contracts, abandoned protocols and assets whose legitimate recovery paths need to be reconstructed.

## Start here

- **[Code](../01-digital-archaeology-code/)** — source archives, tools and test environments
- **[Recovery Maps](../02-recovery-maps/)** — evidence, claimants, recovery conditions and peer review
- **[Summary](../03-summary/)** — findings and publication-ready research overview

## Standards

Every excavation requires a [Recovery Map](./methodology/RECOVERY_MAP_TEMPLATE.md), a [machine-readable record](./methodology/recovery-map.example.json), immutable references and an independent evidence verdict. Do not confuse an asset balance with unencumbered surplus, a simulation with a production transfer, or managerial permissions with beneficial ownership.

Preserved research materials: [Blast source](./blast/), [methodology](./methodology/), [case catalog](./cases/), [evidence standards](./evidence/), [publication work](./publications/), [submissions](../submissions/) and [reviews](../reviews/).
